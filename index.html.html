About

From curiosity to casework

I am a cybersecurity student focused on digital forensics: recovering, analysing and explaining what happened on a compromised system. I enjoy the detective side of security, following artifacts in memory, disk, network traffic and logs until the full story is clear.

I work through hands-on challenges to practise reading packet captures, disk images, memory dumps and logs, and I use platforms like TryHackMe and CyberTalents to keep building those skills step by step. I am not an expert yet, but I am consistent, and I care about doing the work properly.

I also practise ethical hacking in legal lab environments, because understanding how attacks work helps me recognise their traces during an investigation. My goal is to grow into a reliable digital forensics and incident response analyst, and to keep learning from every case I work on.
